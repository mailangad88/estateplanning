## How to use this letter

Naming a guardian in your will is the legal step. This letter is the human step. It tells that person what you know about your children that no court paper can. It is not a legal document. A court generally looks to your will and to what is best for the children, so a letter is guidance, not a command. Rules about guardians vary by state.

Write it in pencil first. Keep it where your guardian can find it, and update it each year. A good time is a birthday, or the first day of school.

Do not put legal wishes here that need to be in your will, such as who the guardian is, or how an inheritance is held. If your letter says something different from your will, the will usually controls.

## Part 1: The basics

- **Guardian's name:** ______________________
- **Backup guardian's name:** ______________________
- **Date of this letter:** ______________________
- **Where my will is kept:** ______________________
- **Attorney or key adviser, phone:** ______________________

## Part 2: Each child

Make one copy of this page for each child.

- **Child's name and birthday:** ______________________
- **Nicknames and what makes them laugh:** ______________________
- **What calms them when they are upset:** ______________________
- **Fears, such as the dark or storms:** ______________________
- **Favorite foods and foods to avoid:** ______________________
- **Bedtime routine, in order:** ______________________
- **Comfort items:** ______________________
- **What they are good at:** ______________________
- **What is hard for them:** ______________________

Sample line: "Maya says she is not tired until she has had her story and her water. Please let her pick the book. She likes to be asked."

## Part 3: Health

- **Doctor, dentist and phones:** ______________________
- **Allergies and medicines:** ______________________
- **Conditions or ongoing care:** ______________________
- **Therapists, other doctors or counselors:** ______________________
- **Insurance information and where the cards are:** ______________________
- **Where vaccination records are kept:** ______________________

Tell your guardian about anything the children may not be able to describe for themselves, such as a sensory need or a medication schedule.

## Part 4: School, activities and friends

- **School, teacher and phone:** ______________________
- **How we handle homework:** ______________________
- **Activities and teams, and the coach's name:** ______________________
- **Closest friends and their parents' names:** ______________________
- **Where the school records are kept:** ______________________

If you have a view about school, such as keeping the children in the same district if possible, say so, and say why. A guardian may not be able to follow it, so give the reasoning.

## Part 5: Values and traditions

Choose a few things that matter most rather than a long list.

- **What I most want my children to know about me:** ______________________
- **Values I hope they carry:** ______________________
- **Faith or cultural practices:** ______________________
- **Holidays and family traditions:** ______________________
- **How we handle discipline:** ______________________
- **Screens, social media and phones:** ______________________

Sample line: "We say what we feel at the dinner table. It can get loud. Please do not punish them for being honest."

## Part 6: People to keep close

Children can lose many connections at once. Naming people helps your guardian keep them.

| Name | Relationship | Contact | How often I hope they stay in touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| ______________ | ______________ | ______________ | ______________ |
| ______________ | ______________ | ______________ | ______________ |
| ______________ | ______________ | ______________ | ______________ |

Include extended family, godparents, friends and anyone from a different side of the family you want them to stay close to.

## Part 7: Money and the home

Your will or trust holds the legal money plan. This part explains your intent in plain terms.

- **How I hope the children's money is used (school, health, a home):** ______________________
- **Who manages the money, if different from the guardian:** ______________________
- **Where the life insurance and accounts are listed:** ______________________
- **What I hope for the family home:** ______________________
- **Expenses I hope the guardian will not hesitate to spend on:** ______________________

If your guardian is not the person managing the money, say that clearly, and tell them how to ask for funds. Our [guardian fund calculator](/tools/guardian-fund-calculator) can help you estimate what a guardian might need.

## Part 8: A note to your guardian

Add a few lines in your own voice.

- **Why I chose you:** ______________________
- **What I hope for you, and for our children:** ______________________
- **Permission: things I want you to feel free to change:** ______________________

Sample line: "You will do things differently from us. That is fine. Please raise them as yourself."

## Review checklist

- [ ] Talked to the guardian and the backup before naming them
- [ ] Told the guardian where this letter is kept
- [ ] Matched this letter against my will
- [ ] Updated phone numbers, school and medical details
- [ ] Set a yearly reminder to review

## When to talk to an attorney

- Your co-parent, a former spouse or a relative may disagree with your choice
- Your children have special needs or ongoing care
- You are not sure how to leave money for your children and the guardian
- You and your spouse live in different states or hold different citizenships
- You have not yet named a guardian in a signed will
