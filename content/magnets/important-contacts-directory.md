**How to use this template.** Fill in the tables, print them, and keep a copy with your documents and one with your executor or agent. Phone numbers change, so include an email and an office address. Add a note about what the person knows. A professional cannot discuss your matters with just anyone, so your agent may need a signed authorization or a legal appointment first.

## Cover note (give this to the person who will use it)

Copy and adapt:

> This directory lists the people who handle my legal, financial and medical matters. Start with the first section if I am ill or cannot make decisions, and the section marked "after a death" if I have died. My original documents are at ____________________. You are named as ____________________. If any of these professionals will not speak to you, ask them what paperwork they need. I trust you to do what you can and to ask for help when you need it.

## Rules of thumb for the person using this

- Call the attorney early. Do not assume anything has to be signed or paid before asking what is needed.
- Ask each professional what documents they need before they will speak with you.
- Keep a call log: date, person, what was said and any promised follow-up.
- Do not give out account numbers or Social Security numbers over a call you did not start. Scammers read obituaries.
- Do not post travel plans or empty-house details online.

## Keeping the directory current

Review it each year with the [plan review reminder](/tools/plan-review-reminder). Update it when someone retires, moves or changes jobs. A directory that sends your agent to a retired attorney is worse than none. Also compare it with the broader [important contacts checklist](/checklists/important-contacts-list).

## When to talk to an attorney

- Your agent does not yet have legal authority to speak with your bank or doctors.
- You cannot name an executor, or the person you chose has not been asked.
- You use several advisers who do not know about each other.
- A professional listed holds your original documents and you are unsure of their policy.
- You have a business and need a plan for key contacts to step in.

## Next step
