**How to use this kit.** Pick a date once a year: a birthday, a tax-filing week, or the start of the year. Book one hour. Use the agenda, answer the questions, and fill in the tracker. Photocopy the tracker each year so you can compare. Who attends is up to you: partners only, or adult children too.

## Invitation wording

Short and low pressure works well.

> "Once a year I would like us to spend an hour making sure our paperwork and our wishes still match. No big decisions required. I will bring coffee and a list."

For a parent:

> "I want to make sure I know where your important papers are and who to call. You decide what you share."

## The one-hour agenda

| Minutes | Topic | Who leads |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | What changed this year? | Anyone |
| 5 to 20 | Documents and beneficiaries | Person who keeps the files |
| 20 to 30 | Accounts, debts and insurance | Person who pays bills |
| 30 to 40 | Health, care and who decides | Everyone |
| 40 to 50 | Family wishes and heirlooms | Everyone |
| 50 to 60 | Update the tracker and set next date | Anyone |

## Part 1: What changed this year?

Tick everything that applies. Each one can affect a plan.

- [ ] Marriage, divorce or separation
- [ ] Birth or adoption
- [ ] Death of a family member, executor, trustee or guardian
- [ ] A child turned 18
- [ ] New job, retirement or business change
- [ ] Bought, sold or refinanced a home
- [ ] Inherited money or property
- [ ] Moved to a new state
- [ ] New diagnosis or care need
- [ ] A person we named no longer seems right

## Part 2: Documents and beneficiaries

## Part 3: Accounts, debts and insurance

- **Accounts opened or closed this year:** ______________________
- **Debts paid off or added:** ______________________
- **Insurance renewals due:** ______________________
- **Anything that changed in value a lot:** ______________________
- **Subscriptions or autopays to cancel:** ______________________

Questions to ask:

- If one of us were gone tomorrow, could the other find every account?
- Do we both know how bills get paid?
- Does anyone else need a login or a copy?

## Part 4: Health and care

- **Health care agent still willing:** ______________________
- **Backup agent:** ______________________
- **Doctor and pharmacy contacts:** ______________________
- **Anything we want to say about care preferences:** ______________________

If someone is aging or ill, ask what help they would want and who is nearby. For this conversation, see [healthcare directives and living wills](/guides/healthcare-directives-and-living-wills).

## Part 5: Family wishes

Pick two or three. Do not rush.

- What is one item you hope stays in the family?
- Is there anything you worry will cause disagreement later?
- Is there anyone we have not talked to who should know the plan?
- Are there gifts or causes we want to support?

Write down a decision, even if it is "we will decide next year."

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Waiting for a crisis. Check-ins work because nothing is at stake.
- Talking only about death. Include health, money management and caregiving.
- Treating the meeting as a lecture. Let each person talk first.
- Updating documents but not forms. Both need attention.
- Forgetting the originals. Know where they are.

## Setting a reminder

Put the next date in a calendar with a repeat. The [plan review reminder](/tools/plan-review-reminder) can help, and the [annual estate plan review checklist](/checklists/annual-estate-plan-review) goes deeper into the paperwork side.

## When to talk to an attorney

- A birth, marriage, divorce, death or move happened since you last signed.
- Your tracker shows a document older than five years with no review.
- A beneficiary or named person is no longer right.
- You own a business or property in more than one state.
- Your family cannot agree on something important.
