**How to use this worksheet.** Spend one evening with last month's bank and credit card statements. Highlight every recurring charge and copy it into the tables. Do not write passwords or full account numbers on it. Note where the login lives instead. The goal is that a spouse, adult child or agent could keep essential bills paid for three months without calling you.

## Keep it alive

Revisit when you change banks, get a new card, buy a home, retire or hit a life event. Set a twice-yearly reminder. A stale map can be worse than none, since it tells an agent to look in an account that is closed.

## When to talk to an attorney

- You pay household bills alone and have no power of attorney.
- A family member would need legal authority to access your accounts.
- You have joint debts or co-signed loans and want to know exposure.
- You support someone who receives public benefits.
- You expect to be away for long periods or face a serious diagnosis.
