## How to use this guide

We are sorry for your loss. McHenry County is the 22nd Judicial Circuit of Illinois, with its courthouse in Woodstock. This guide covers the usual steps and what the county posts about them. Confirm details with the clerk or an attorney, since forms and rooms change. This is education, not legal advice.

## Does the estate belong in McHenry County?

Illinois sends a probate case to the county where the person had a known place of residence (755 ILCS 5/5-1). If the person lived in McHenry County, in a town such as Crystal Lake, McHenry, Woodstock or Algonquin, the case belongs here. The county clerk's probate page says the person must have lived in McHenry County. If the person lived outside Illinois, the rules look to where their Illinois real estate or personal property sits, so ask an attorney.

Next, ask whether a court case is needed at all. A small estate affidavit may work when personal property is $150,000 or less for a death on or after August 15, 2025. Real estate in one person's name, or a family dispute, usually means a court case. See the [small estate affidavit guide](/free/illinois-small-estate-affidavit-guide).

## Where to go

The Michael J. Sullivan Judicial Center is at 2200 North Seminary Avenue, Woodstock, IL 60098. The county lists the main phone as (815) 334-4000 and says court sessions are held Monday through Friday. Probate is handled through the Circuit Clerk's civil division. The county's probate page names Room 136 on the first floor for filing, and public viewing terminals in Rooms 136 and 353. The page also names the McHenry County Government Center, so confirm the building and room with the clerk before you go.

## Local notes

- **Filing the will:** The clerk's page says a will must be filed within 30 days after the date of death, and that the original must be delivered to the Circuit Clerk. State law says to file it promptly. Do not wait.
- **No filing before death:** The clerk will not accept a will before the person has died.
- **Petition and will together:** The clerk says the petition and the original will may be filed at the same time.
- **Small estate form:** The McHenry County Circuit Clerk posts a small estate affidavit form revised August 15, 2025. Confirm with the institutions holding the assets that they accept the form.
- **Self-help:** Forms are available online and through a room the page lists as Room 356.

## The Illinois steps that apply here

These steps come from the state Probate Act, so they are the same in every county.

| Step | What happens | Rule to know |
|---|---|---|
| File the will | The person holding the original gives it to the circuit clerk | Do it promptly. Hiding a will for 30 days after learning of the death can be a felony (755 ILCS 5/6-1) |
| Notice to heirs | Heirs and legatees get a copy of the petition and the order | Within 14 days after the order (755 ILCS 5/6-10) |
| Notice to creditors | Published once a week for three weeks in a county newspaper, and mailed to known creditors | Claim date is the later of six months from first publication or three months from mailing (755 ILCS 5/18-3) |
| Inventory | A verified list of assets is filed | Within 60 days after letters issue (755 ILCS 5/14-1) |
| Outer claim limit | Late claims are cut off | Two years after death (755 ILCS 5/18-12) |

For family members, an interested person can ask for formal proof of the will within 42 days after it is admitted (755 ILCS 5/6-21). A will contest must be filed within six months after admission (755 ILCS 5/8-1). Our [Illinois probate checklist](/free/illinois-probate-checklist) walks through every stage.

## When to talk to an attorney

- The will names no executor, or the person named cannot serve
- A relative may contest the will or disagrees about who should serve
- The estate owns real estate, a business or property in another state
- You are not sure the small estate affidavit is allowed
- A creditor sends a claim you do not understand
- You are asked to sign court papers and are not sure what they do
