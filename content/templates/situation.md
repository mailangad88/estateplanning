# Template: situation

Target: a reader's circumstance or audience. Length 1,200 to 2,000 words.

- **Title:** "Estate planning for <audience>: <the specific problem they have>".
- **answer:** the two or three things that are different for this reader.
- **Sections:**
  1. Why this situation changes the usual plan (the specific risk).
  2. The documents and tools that matter most for them, each linked to its guide.
  3. A checklist for this month.
  4. A realistic example.
  5. `## How we can help`
- Route heavily into the topic clusters. This page is a hub for its audience.
- Keep any health, disability or sexual orientation pages out of ad pixels and remarketing (backlog B10).
