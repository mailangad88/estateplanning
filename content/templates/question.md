# Template: question

Target: one specific question, phrased the way people type it. Length 900 to 1,500 words.

- **Title:** the question itself, or the question plus a short qualifier ("Can an executor take money out of the estate? What's allowed and what isn't").
- **answer:** a direct yes/no/it-depends-on-X answer in the first sentence, then the one or two conditions that change it.
- **Sections:**
  1. `## The short version` with 3 to 5 bullets a reader can act on.
  2. `## When the answer is yes` / `## When the answer is no` (or the conditions that decide it), each with a realistic example.
  3. `## What to do if ...` with numbered steps for the person in the situation.
  4. `## How state law changes this` naming the kind of rule that varies, linking state guides.
  5. `## How we can help`
- **FAQs:** 4 to 6 follow-up questions, never repeating one used on another page.
