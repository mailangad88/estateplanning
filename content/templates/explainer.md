# Template: explainer

Target: a concept, document or process. Length 1,200 to 2,000 words.

- **Title:** "What is X?" or "X: how it works, step by step" or a checklist title.
- **answer:** a plain definition plus why it matters to the reader.
- **Sections:**
  1. What it is, in plain words, with the glossary term linked.
  2. How it works: a numbered process or timeline.
  3. A table (who does what, documents needed, or timeline by stage).
  4. Common mistakes and how to avoid them.
  5. A worked example with invented first names.
  6. `## How we can help`
