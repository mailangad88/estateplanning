# Template: cost

Target: "How much does X cost". Length 1,200 to 1,800 words.

- **answer:** a typical national range and the two or three things that move a price up or down.
- **Ranges:** give typical ranges only, labelled as typical. Never invent a survey or average. Cite a named survey only with a link. Never quote the firm's own prices unless they are in `src/config/firm.ts`.
- **Sections:**
  1. A table of price drivers (complexity, state, attorney vs. online, add-ons).
  2. What's usually included in a flat fee, and what costs extra.
  3. The cost of not doing it (probate, court fees, family conflict), stated carefully.
  4. Questions to ask about fees before hiring anyone.
  5. `## How we can help` (flat-fee quote before anything is signed).
