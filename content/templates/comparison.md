# Template: comparison

Target: "X vs. Y". Length 1,200 to 1,800 words.

- **Title:** "X vs. Y: differences, costs and which fits your situation".
- **answer:** the core difference in one sentence, then who usually picks each.
- **Sections:**
  1. A comparison table: purpose, cost range, court involvement, privacy, control, when it takes effect, who it suits.
  2. `## When X makes more sense` and `## When Y makes more sense`, each with an example.
  3. `## Can you use both?` (often yes; explain how they fit together).
  4. Mistakes people make choosing between them.
  5. `## How we can help` linking the relevant tool (e.g. /tools/will-or-trust).
- Be even-handed. Never imply the more expensive option is always better.
