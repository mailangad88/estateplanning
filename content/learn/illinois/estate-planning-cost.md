If you are comparing an online form, a neighborhood attorney's hourly bill and a flat-fee package, the hard part is that each one counts a different set of things. This page puts our published prices next to the other options so you can compare like for like.

It is the Illinois companion to our national guide on [how much an estate plan costs](/learn/basics/how-much-does-estate-planning-cost). Everything here sits within our [Illinois estate planning guide](/learn/illinois). ## What are the published prices?

| Package | Price | Who it fits | Revisions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essentials | $2,500 | One person or a couple with modest assets and no minor children | One round |
| Complete | $3,500 | Homeowners and families with minor children, the usual fit for families with a home | One round |
| Legacy | Custom quote | Blended families, business owners, property in more than one state, special needs planning or larger estates | Set in the quote |

Each price is a flat fee for the plan, for one person or a couple. Quotes for Legacy come after a first conversation about what you own and who you want to protect.

## What does each package include?

**Essentials ($2,500).** A will for each spouse, guardian nominations if you have children, a durable financial power of attorney, a healthcare power of attorney with a living will and HIPAA release, a signing ceremony and one round of revisions.

**Complete ($3,500).** A revocable living trust, a pour-over will, financial and healthcare powers of attorney, a living will and HIPAA release, a deed moving your home into the trust, a funding checklist plus one review, a signing ceremony and one round of revisions.

**Legacy (custom).** Everything in Complete, plus special needs or children's trust provisions, additional deeds, a business interest assignment and coordination with your financial advisor or CPA.

Essentials leaves out trust drafting, real estate deeds and tax planning. Complete leaves out tax planning beyond the trust and deeds not listed in the custom scope. No package includes court proceedings. Revisions after the first round are not included in Essentials or Complete.

## What does estate planning cost with other options?

We do not publish other providers' prices, because they change and many are bundled. What you can compare is the structure.

| Option | How it is priced | What you give up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Online form service | A low fixed fee or subscription for generated documents | Nobody checks your beneficiary forms, deed or family situation |
| Hourly attorney | Time spent, billed in increments | A cost you cannot predict before you start |
| Flat-fee attorney | One price for a defined set of documents | Work outside the scope is quoted separately |
| Do it yourself | Your time and a form | Signing mistakes show up only after you die |

A form can be a sensible stopgap, and our [online will versus estate attorney comparison](/compare/online-will-vs-estate-attorney) is fair about when. If you do use one, make sure it is signed the way [Illinois requires](/learn/illinois/illinois-will-requirements): in writing, with two credible witnesses watching you sign.

## What drives the price up or down in Illinois?

- **A home.** Moving a house into a trust takes a new deed and recording. That is why the home-owner package costs more than the will-only one.
- **Children.** Minor children add guardian choices and, often, trust terms for their money.
- **A blended family.** Second marriages and stepchildren need more careful drafting, which is where a custom quote starts.
- **A business or rental property.** Interests in an LLC or a second property add deeds and assignments.
- **Property in another state.** See [owning property outside Illinois](/learn/illinois/illinois-resident-owning-property-in-another-state).
- **Taxes.** Illinois has its own estate tax at a lower threshold than the federal one. See the [Illinois estate tax guide](/learn/illinois/illinois-estate-tax) if your estate is large.

Not everyone needs a trust. A single person who rents and has no children may be well served by Essentials. A homeowner with young children usually benefits from Complete. A [living trust in Illinois](/learn/illinois/illinois-living-trusts) avoids probate for what is retitled into it and for nothing else, so it earns its price mostly for people with a home.

## Is a cheaper plan really cheaper?

Look at what can happen on the other side. Probate costs fall on your family, not on you. In Cook County the clerk lists a $479 filing fee for an independent administration as of October 2025, and that is only one line. Newspaper notice, a bond premium and attorney time come on top, as the [Illinois probate costs](/learn/illinois/illinois-probate-costs) page lays out. A family dealing with a will that was signed wrong, or a house never deeded into a trust, pays those costs and spends months on them.

Take Priya, 41, who buys a trust kit for her Evanston condo. She signs it but never records a deed. Years later her son opens a probate case to sell the condo. In this example, the money she saved is spent several times over on filing fees, publication and attorney time, and her son waits for letters of office before he can act. Our [probate cost estimator](/tools/probate-cost-estimator) shows how those items add up.

None of this means a form is always a mistake. It means that a document is only worth what it does when your family needs it. The practical test is simple. Ask whether a stranger, a bank officer or a county recorder would accept the paper without questions. If the answer depends on details only a person who has seen your accounts would know, have someone look at it before you rely on it.

Cost also has a timing side. A plan drafted this month costs the same whether or not you are healthy. A plan attempted after a diagnosis, or after a parent starts forgetting things, often costs more in stress and sometimes cannot be done at all, because signing a will or a power of attorney requires the capacity to understand what you are signing. That is a reason to treat the price as a fixed, known amount now rather than an open-ended one later.

## What questions should you ask about any fee?

1. What documents are in the price, and which are not?
2. Is it one fee for a couple, or one per person?
3. How many rounds of revisions are included, and what do extra ones cost?
4. Is the deed for my home included, and who pays the recording fee?
5. Who will review my beneficiary forms?
6. What happens if my life changes in a year?
7. Will I get the price in writing before I sign?
