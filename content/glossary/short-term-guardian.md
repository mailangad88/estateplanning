Section 11-5.4 of the Probate Act (755 ILCS 5/11-5.4) lets a parent sign a written declaration naming a short-term guardian, with authority lasting up to 365 days unless the parent sets a shorter period. No court filing is needed. The guardian can handle school, medical care, and daily needs for the child. The tool suits travel, a hospital stay, or military service. A parent may renew it by signing a new declaration. The declaration does not give the guardian custody beyond its term or the right to move the child permanently.

## Example

Paulo, a pilot, signs a declaration naming his brother Lee as short-term guardian of his two children while he travels for 40 days. A school nurse and pediatrician accept Lee's copy without needing any court order.

## Why it matters

A short-term guardian is quick and cheap, but it is not a substitute for a will naming a permanent [Guardian](/glossary/guardian). Keep copies with the school and doctors.
