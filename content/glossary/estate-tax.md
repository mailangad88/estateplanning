The tax is paid by the estate, not by heirs, before assets are distributed. Each person has a basic exclusion amount, $15,000,000 in 2026, indexed for inflation after 2026. A married couple can shelter $30 million using [Portability](/glossary/portability) and the [Unlimited marital deduction](/glossary/marital-deduction). Property left to a spouse who is a U.S. citizen and gifts to charity are generally deductible. The estate files Form 706 if its size, or the use of portability, requires it. Some states have their own estate tax with much lower thresholds.

## Example

Walter, a widower, dies in 2026 with a $17,000,000 estate. The first $15,000,000 is sheltered, so the taxable amount is $2,000,000 and the federal tax is about 40 percent of that, or roughly $800,000, before deductions and credits. A neighbor with a $4,000,000 estate owes no federal tax.

## Why it matters

Most families will never owe federal estate tax, but state-level taxes and tax filing rules can still apply, and the numbers can change with new laws. The "as of 2026" figures above are worth checking each year. A surviving spouse may need to file even when no tax is due to preserve a deceased spouse's unused exclusion.
