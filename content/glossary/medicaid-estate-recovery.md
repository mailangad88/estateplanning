Federal law requires every state to seek repayment from the estates of Medicaid recipients who received long-term care after age 55 or while permanently institutionalized. In Illinois the Department of Healthcare and Family Services files a claim against the estate (305 ILCS 5/5-13). Recovery is deferred while a spouse, a child under 21, or a blind or disabled child survives. For deaths on or after July 1, 2022, the first $25,000 of the estate is protected. Heirs are not personally liable beyond what the estate holds.

## Example

Edna dies on Medicaid after three years in a nursing home that cost the program $140,000. Her probate estate is a $95,000 house and $5,000 in cash. After funeral and legal costs, the state can claim from what is left above the $25,000 protection.

## Why it matters

Estate recovery is a reason to understand how title and beneficiary forms work before a nursing home is needed. It generally reaches the probate estate.
