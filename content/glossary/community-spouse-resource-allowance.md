Federal spousal impoverishment rules let the spouse at home, called the community spouse, keep half of the couple's countable assets, within a minimum and a maximum that change each January. In 2025 the federal maximum was $157,920. Illinois applies these limits when its Medicaid agency, the Department of Healthcare and Family Services, counts assets as of the first continuous stay in a facility. The home, one car, and household goods are generally not counted. The community spouse may also keep a minimum monthly income allowance. Check current figures before planning.

## Example

Anton enters a nursing home and the couple's countable assets are $240,000. His wife Greta keeps half, or $120,000, which sits between the minimum and the maximum. Anton must spend or protect the other $120,000 down to the $17,500 asset limit that he can keep.

## Why it matters

Without this allowance, a healthy spouse could be left with almost nothing. The numbers are updated yearly and the rules interact with the [Medicaid look-back period](/glossary/look-back-period).
