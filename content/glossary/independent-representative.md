Under 755 ILCS 5/28-1 and the sections that follow, the court grants independent administration at the time it issues letters or later by petition. The representative then has broad powers to manage, lease, sell, and invest estate property without further orders, subject to any limits in the will. The representative must still give notice to heirs and legatees, publish the creditor notice, keep estate funds apart from personal funds, and account at the end. The court keeps power to review the work if someone complains.

## Example

Nadia is named executor and granted independent administration. Eleven months in, one heir asks the court to review her handling of a $12,000 repair bill. The judge can hear the dispute even though the case is independent.

## Why it matters

The title sounds like total freedom, but the duties of a [Personal representative](/glossary/personal-representative) remain. Personal liability for losses is possible if an independent representative ignores them.
