## Where each one goes

- **Social:** tools 1 to 3 as screen-recording posts (show the tool, show the result, no voiceover needed). Guides as carousels made from their own headings.
- **Partner emails:** CPAs get 2, 3, 10. Advisors get 10, 12, pricing. Realtors get the clear-title guide and 8. Senior living gets 11 and 12. Funeral homes get 5, 6, and the executor checklist.
- **Google Business Profile posts:** one resource per post, twice a week.
- **Meta ads (if approved):** 4, 11 and 13 as email-signup offers. Never promote 5 to 8 as ads to people in grief; let those be found through search.
- **Talks:** print 4 and 11 as take-home sheets.

## Link format

Add tracking every time: `?utm_source=<where>&utm_medium=<social|email|partner|print>&utm_campaign=<post or batch id>`.
For printed handouts use a short readable path and `utm_medium=print`, or a QR code that carries the tags.
