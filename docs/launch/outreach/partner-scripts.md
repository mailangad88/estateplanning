# Referral partner outreach: scripts for Illinois

For CPAs, financial advisors, realtors, senior living and home care, funeral homes, and community venues.
Drafts only. Sargun approves before the first send. Log every touch the same day in `research/partner-kit/partner-tracker-template.csv`.

These are short versions written for the referral-site model and for Illinois. The longer per-partner kits in `research/partner-kit/` (handouts, talk outlines, objection answers) still apply; swap "the firm" for "Family Future Planning" and "our attorney" for "an Illinois estate planning attorney in our network".

## Ground rules (say them out loud, they help)

- We never pay for a referral and never accept payment. No per-lead fees, no gift cards, no "marketing fee". Token holiday gifts only, logged, never tied to a referral.
- Every client someone refers is told who referred them. The arrangement is not exclusive.
- We never tell a partner whether someone contacted us or hired an attorney unless that person signs a release.
- The partner's own compliance rules come first. Offer to send materials to their compliance team.
- Public partner rules: https://familyfutureplanning.com/partners

Who sends: Angad can send these as the business. The responsible attorney (Sargun) is named in the signature. Calls from partners go to Sargun's line once it is on the site.

## The 5-touch sequence (about 17 days)

| Touch | Day | Channel | Script |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Email | Section 1 to 5 (by partner type) |
| 2 | 3 | LinkedIn connect | L2 in `announcement-emails.md` |
| 3 | 6 | Call or voicemail | Section 6 |
| 4 | 10 | Follow-up email | Section 6 |
| 5 | 17 | Invite (coffee, lunch-and-learn, or library talk) | Section 7 |

Stop after touch 5 if there is no answer. A no is a no.

Rule for every first email: the first line is one real, checkable thing about them (a post they wrote, a service they offer, their neighborhood). If you can't find one, don't send yet. Generic emails get ignored and burn the contact.

## 4. Senior living, home care, geriatric care managers

Ask for the community life director, the marketing or admissions director, or the social worker. Mid-week, mid-morning. Never ask for access to residents or family lists. Offer a talk instead.

**Subject:** A free talk for residents' families on powers of attorney

> Hi [first name],
>
> [real line: "I saw [community] hosts a monthly family night."]
>
> Families who move a parent into [community] often find out late that there's no power of attorney for health care or for finances. By then, signing one may not be possible, and a court guardianship is slow and costly.
>
> I run Family Future Planning, which helps Illinois families get these documents in place with an estate planning attorney. We'd be glad to give a 30-minute plain-English talk for residents and families: "The three papers every family should find before a crisis." No selling at the talk, just a checklist people take home.
>
> Free Illinois guides we'd hand out:
> https://familyfutureplanning.com/free/illinois-health-care-poa-guide?utm_source=partner&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=senior
>
> Would a date in December or January work?
>
> [signature as above]

At the talk: give the talk, hand out the checklist, answer questions people ask, and let them contact you. Don't approach individual attendees to pitch (Illinois Rule 7.3).

## 7. Library, senior center, park district and Rotary talk pitch

Free venues, real audiences. Many suburban libraries run adult programs; ask the adult services librarian.

**Subject:** Program idea: "Illinois wills and probate in plain English" (free)

> Hi [name],
>
> I'd like to offer a free 45-minute program for [library] patrons: "Wills, trusts and probate in Illinois, in plain English." It covers who inherits without a will under Illinois law, when probate can be skipped, and the three documents every adult should have. Every attendee gets a printed checklist.
>
> There's no sales pitch at the program. I run Family Future Planning, an Illinois estate planning referral site, and an Illinois estate planning attorney would co-present. We follow your rules on promotional material.
>
> Would a weeknight in January or February work? Happy to work around your calendar.
>
> [signature as above]

Local press angle (patch.com community posts, local papers' community calendars): list each scheduled talk as a free community event. Calendar listings are free and get read.

## 8. Thank-you notes

After a referral. Never name the client or say what happened.

> Hi [first name], thank you for thinking of us. It means a lot this early on. If you ever want more of the checklists for your office, just say the word.

After a talk or a meeting:

> Thanks for the time today, [first name]. I'm sending the [handout / slides] as promised. What you said about [specific thing] is going into our next guide.
