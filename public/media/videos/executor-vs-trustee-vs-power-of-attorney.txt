Executor vs trustee vs power of attorney: who does what

Three roles, three different jobs. People mix them up all the time. An executor handles your estate after you die. They file the will, pay debts, and distribute what's left. A trustee manages what's inside a trust, for you while you are alive, and for your beneficiaries later. A power of attorney lets an agent act for you while you're alive, usually for money and property. It ends at death. A separate health care directive names who speaks for you on medical choices. Choose people who are honest and organized, and name backups. Our guide helps.
