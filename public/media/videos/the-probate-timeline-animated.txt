The probate timeline, animated

Probate is the court process of settling someone's estate. Timelines vary a lot, but here is the usual path. First, the will, if there is one, is filed, and the court appoints a personal representative. That often takes a few weeks. Next, creditors and heirs are notified. There is a set window for creditor claims, often several months. The representative inventories assets, may order appraisals, and pays valid debts and taxes. A final income tax return is needed for the person who died. Sometimes the estate itself has to file returns too. When the court is satisfied, assets go to the people named in the will, or to heirs under state law. Then the estate is closed. A simple one can take under a year.
