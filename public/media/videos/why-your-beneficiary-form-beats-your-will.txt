Why your beneficiary form beats your will

Your will says one thing. Your beneficiary form says another. Which wins? For retirement accounts and life insurance, usually the form. Those assets pass to whoever is named, no matter what the will says. That means an old form can send money to a former spouse, or leave out a child. Check each account. Name a backup. Update after marriage, divorce, births, and deaths. Our checklist walks you through it. It takes ten minutes.
