Estate tax thresholds explained

Most estates owe no federal estate tax. The threshold is high, and for 2026 it is fifteen million dollars per person. Only the amount above that is taxed, at rates that top out at forty percent. For married couples, portability lets a surviving spouse use the unused part of the first spouse's exemption, but you generally have to file a return to elect it. Gifts during life count against the same exemption, but each year you can give a set amount per person without it counting. For 2026, that is nineteen thousand dollars. Some states have their own estate or inheritance tax, often with much lower thresholds. Check your state's rules. Thresholds change with law and inflation, so check the year.
