Guardianship for minor children: how courts decide

If something happened to both parents, who would raise your children? If you haven't said, a court decides. It looks at what's in the child's best interests, often starting with relatives. Courts usually give weight to the guardian you name in your will, though they still review it. Name a backup. Pick someone whose values and home you trust, not just a favorite relative. Separate from guardian of the person, you can set up money management, often through a trust, so the guardian doesn't have to ask a court for funds.
