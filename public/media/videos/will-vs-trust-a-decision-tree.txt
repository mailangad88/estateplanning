Will vs trust: a decision tree

Do you need a will, a trust, or both? Four questions can point you in the right direction. Question one. Do you have minor children? If yes, a will matters, because it is where you name a guardian. Question two. Do you own real estate, or want to avoid probate? A funded living trust may help. In some states, probate is light, so it depends. Question three. Do you want control over timing, like money at certain ages, or care for a family member with a disability? A trust gives more control. Question four. Is privacy important? A will becomes a public record in probate. A trust usually stays private. Even with a trust, most people still sign a simple will, called a pour-over will, plus powers of attorney.
