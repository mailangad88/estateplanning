The Medicaid five-year look-back, explained

If you may need nursing home care, Medicaid looks back five years at your gifts. Medicare doesn't pay for long-term care. Medicaid does, but only when your assets and income are low. If you gave away assets in that window before applying, Medicaid can set a penalty period when it won't pay. Gifts to family, retitling a home, even large cash transfers may count. There are exceptions, for example for a spouse or a disabled child. They are technical and vary by state. Planning early gives more choices than planning in a crisis. Don't move assets before talking to an elder law attorney in your state. Our guide explains the basics.
