Funding your trust: the six assets to retitle

You signed the trust. Great. But a trust only controls what is in it. One. Your home. This usually means a new deed, recorded in the county where the property sits. Two. Bank accounts. Many banks retitle the account, or add a payable-on-death name, depending on the plan. Three. Brokerage accounts. Typically you retitle them to the trust with your broker. Four. Retirement accounts. These usually stay out of the trust. You name the beneficiary on the form. Ask before changing anything, because of taxes. Five. Life insurance. Review who is the beneficiary, and whether the trust should be. Six. Everything else, like furniture, jewelry, and keepsakes. A short assignment document can cover these. Our checklist tracks all six. Download it and bring it to your next meeting.
