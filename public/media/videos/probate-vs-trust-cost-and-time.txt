Probate vs trust: cost and time comparison

Probate or trust? Here is a plain comparison. Time. Probate is court-supervised, so it often takes months to over a year. A trust can usually be settled faster, since no court has to approve each step. Cost. Probate can mean court fees and attorney fees set by statute or by hourly rate, depending on the state. A trust costs more to set up, but often less to administer. Privacy. Probate filings are public. Trust administration usually stays private. Effort. A trust has to be funded and kept up to date.
